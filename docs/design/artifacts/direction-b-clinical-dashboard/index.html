05 · Active study question with explanation
Study Item 8 of 25

cctc-2004 · Domain 2

When a transplant program declines a deceased-donor kidney offer presented through DonorNet, what documentation must be provided to UNOS?

Correct — A

Whenever a deceased-donor kidney offer is declined, a reason or refusal code must be provided to UNOS even though the accepting physician makes the final decision.

References

  1. Handbook of Kidney Transplantation, 6th ed. (Danovitch, 2017). Ch. 5 → DonorNet paragraph (“whenever an offer is declined, a reason or ‘refusal code’ must be provided to UNOS”); PDF p. 113.
  2. OPTN/UNOS — Policy 18.3: Recording and Reporting the Outcomes of Organ Offers (HRSA). Organ-offer refusal reasons reported in DonorNet.
  3. OPTN/UNOS — Policies (HRSA). Policy 18.3 → Recording and Reporting the Outcomes of Organ Offers (PTR refusal codes for declined organ offers); PDF p. 334.
