05 · Active study question with explanation
Study Item 12 of 25

cctc-1081 · Domain 1

When counseling a transplant candidate about pretransplant immunizations, why is early vaccination emphasized in standard references?

Correct — A

Candidates and recipients are at increased infection risk; response to many vaccines is diminished in organ failure, and transplant recipients may have suboptimal vaccination responses due to immunosuppression, so immunizations should be updated before transplantation when possible.

References

  1. Core Curriculum for Transplant Nurses, 2nd ed. (Cupples et al., 2016). Ch. 5 → Immunizations → A.2.–4.; PDF p. 180.
